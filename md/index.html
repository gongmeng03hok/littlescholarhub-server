Soft mode, everywhere

Every detail tuned down, on purpose.

Soft mode isn't a filter over a bright app — it's the only mode. Lower contrast, warmer neutrals, and gentle motion, from the first screen to the last.

🌙

Muted by default

No pure white, no pure black. Every surface sits on a lavender-grey base so late-night eyes never brace for glare.

🌿

Three gentle prompts

What went well, what felt heavy, what you're carrying into tomorrow. Nothing else competes for your attention.

🕯️

A closing ritual

The breathing orb slows as you write. When it settles, your entry is saved — a small, visible sign that you're done for the day.
